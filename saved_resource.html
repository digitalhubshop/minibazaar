Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?
Once your order is placed, you can track it in real-time from the "My Orders" section in your profile. You'll also receive notifications at each stage.
What payment methods are supported?
We support UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and other popular payment methods through our secure payment gateway.
How does the return policy work?
Most products come with a 7-day easy return policy. If you're not satisfied, you can request a return from your order details page.
How can I become a seller?
Click "Become a Seller" and complete the registration flow. Once your account is verified, you can start listing products immediately.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use a PCI-compliant payment gateway. We never store your card details, CVV, or UPI PIN on our servers.
